How a run works

You are a travelling bookseller with a book-summoning cat. Sell to the strange folk of the Underhive, beat each Stage's Boss, and keep going down.

  • Pick 2 TraitsThey set your 11-book deck and which new books you can find
  • 5 ReputationEach visitor you fail costs 1. A Boss win refills it
  • 4 books, 3 ActionsThat's your hand and your moves each turn at the counter
  • Loot is run-onlyGold, books and Curios last one run. Wins unlock things for good

Inside a Stage

Each Stage has its own map. Move left to right, choosing one stop at a time.

  • BooksellingServe a crowd, win rewards
  • DistinguishedHarder crowd, better loot
  • EventA story with a choice
  • MerchantBuy books, Curios and services
  • FindingSmall loot: Gold, Fragments
  • TreasureTake 1 of 2 spoils
  • Curio ShrineTrade Fragments for Curios
  • BossWin to go one Stage deeper

Loot you spend along the way

  • SummonSee 2 new books from your Traits, keep 1
  • Rebinding BaseUpgrade one book: pick 1 of 2 upgrades
  • Curio FragmentsOffer them at the Shrine
  • CuriosLasting boosts for the rest of the run

At the counter

Play books onto visitors to fill each one's Need before they lose patience.

  1. Need: fill the bar to serve them
  2. Patience: turns left before they walk out
  3. Actions: 3 per turn
  4. Your hand: 4 books, each with a Value
  5. Reputation: each walkout costs 1
The run ends if Reputation hits 0 or a Boss walks out unserved Books come back after every visitor

How you progress

Runs don't make you stronger. Every run starts from the same 11 books, and there is nothing to grind for. Beating a Difficulty is an achievement, earned with what you know and what you choose.

Reset every run

  • Your deck, back to 11 books
  • Curios
  • Fragments
  • Gold, Summons, Rebinds
  • Reputation

Kept for good

  • ✓Difficulties won
  • New Traits and their books
  • Legendary Curios
  • Mythic books

No permanent upgrades, and no currency that carries between runs.

What wins a run

  • Knowing the gameThe visitors, their abilities, what each book really does
  • DeckbuildingWhich Summons to take, what to Rebind, what to skip
  • Curio luckWhat the Shrine and Merchant offer, and which you keep
  • Your choicesWhich road, which stop, which crowd to serve

The game gets harder with every Difficulty. You start each one with more Fragments for stronger Curios, and unlock stronger Curios and Mythic books. Replayability comes from new choices and new unlocks.

The Difficulty ladder

Win a full run to open the next Difficulty. Higher Difficulties start you with more Curio Fragments, but also with cursed books in your run. The first time you win each one, you unlock something for good.
